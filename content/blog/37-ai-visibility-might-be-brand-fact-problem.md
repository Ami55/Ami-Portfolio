# Your AI Visibility Problem Might Be a Brand Fact Problem

When a brand is described incorrectly in an AI answer, the immediate reaction is often, “The model hallucinated.” Sometimes it did. Sometimes the brand’s own website gave it several conflicting versions of the truth.

I have seen company names written differently across pages, outdated service descriptions, old leadership details, locations that appear and disappear, and About pages that do not match product pages.

## Retrieval needs a stable version of the brand

AI systems do not experience a website as a loyal customer does. They retrieve passages, compare sources, and try to connect names, attributes, and relationships.

If the same entity is described five ways, the system has more work to do. If external profiles repeat an old fact, confidence becomes even harder.

The fix is not to repeat the brand name unnaturally. It is to maintain a reliable source of truth.

## The audit starts with ordinary facts

I check the preferred name, short description, services, locations, leadership, founding information, contact details, and important relationships. Then I compare those facts across key pages, schema, social profiles, business listings, and other controlled sources.

This recurring work led to my [AI Hallucination Brand Fact Checker](https://seogirl-ai-hallucination-brand-fact-checker.vercel.app/). The tool helps expose contradictions; a person still needs to decide which statement is correct.

## What I would check on Monday

- Write one approved brand fact sheet.
- Compare it with the About, contact, product, and location pages.
- Review Organization schema and controlled external profiles.
- Remove or explain conflicting legacy information.
- Assign ownership for keeping important facts current.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can inconsistent website facts cause incorrect AI answers?**
They can contribute to uncertainty. AI systems also use external sources, so consistency cannot guarantee a correct answer, but it gives the brand a clearer factual foundation.

**Is this only an AI search issue?**
No. Customers, journalists, partners, search engines, and support teams also depend on consistent brand information.

**Should every page repeat the same brand description?**
No. Context can change the wording, while core facts and entity relationships should remain consistent.
